If your idea of "deep cleaning" is just vacuuming a little harder than usual, you're missing most of what actually needs attention in your home.

Regular cleaning — wiping counters, vacuuming floors, taking out the trash — keeps a home looking presentable day to day. Deep cleaning is a different task altogether: it targets the buildup that accumulates behind, under, and inside things you don't touch every week. The tricky part is that there's no single answer to "how often," because different rooms and surfaces get dirty at different rates.

## Regular Cleaning vs. Deep Cleaning

Regular cleaning handles surface-level dirt and clutter. Deep cleaning goes further — think scrubbing grout, degreasing the stovetop hood, washing baseboards, cleaning behind appliances, and getting into corners that a quick tidy-up never reaches. A home can look clean on the surface and still be overdue for a deep clean.

A good general rule: most homes benefit from a full deep clean every 3 to 6 months, with certain high-use areas needing attention more often. Households with pets, young kids, allergies, or heavy foot traffic should lean toward the more frequent end of that range.

## Room-by-Room Deep Cleaning Frequency

### Kitchen: Every 1–3 Months

The kitchen accumulates grease, food splatter, and bacteria faster than any other room. Grease from cooking settles on cabinet fronts, the range hood, and the backsplash even if you wipe counters daily. Aim to deep clean the oven, degrease the stovetop and hood, and clean out the fridge every one to three months, and don't forget to pull out light appliances to clean underneath and behind them.

### Bathroom: Every 1–2 Months

Humidity makes bathrooms a prime spot for mold and mildew, particularly in grout lines, shower corners, and around the base of the toilet. Dust mites and mildew both thrive in damp environments, so a deep scrub of tile grout, the showerhead, and exhaust fan covers every month or two keeps things sanitary, not just tidy-looking.

### Bedrooms: Every 3–4 Months

Bedrooms need less frequent deep cleaning, but mattresses, under-bed areas, and closets collect dust over time. Rotating and vacuuming your mattress, washing pillows, and clearing dust from vents and ceiling fixtures every few months makes a noticeable difference in air quality, especially during allergy season.

### Living Room: Every 3–4 Months

Upholstery, curtains, and carpets trap dust, pet dander, and odours. A deep clean here means vacuuming under furniture, cleaning upholstery, washing or steaming curtains, and addressing carpet stains that a quick vacuum won't lift.

### Windows and Baseboards: Every 3–6 Months

These are the areas people forget most. Window tracks collect grime, and baseboards act like a dust magnet along every wall in the house. If you live in a house rather than a condo, it's also worth checking window sills and entryways after winter — road salt and slush get tracked in and can leave a dusty residue that lingers well into spring.

## A Simple Seasonal Deep Cleaning Checklist

Use this as a starting point and adjust the frequency to your household's needs:

- **Kitchen**: oven, stovetop hood, fridge interior, cabinet fronts, backsplash
- **Bathroom**: grout, showerhead, exhaust fan, behind and under the toilet
- **Bedrooms**: mattress, closet shelves, ceiling fixtures, under the bed
- **Living areas**: upholstery, curtains, carpets, vents
- **Whole home**: baseboards, window tracks, door frames, light switches
- **Appliances**: behind and underneath the fridge, washer, and dryer

## Factors That Change the Schedule

A few things push deep cleaning higher on the priority list:

- **Pets** shed and track in dirt, meaning carpets and upholstery need attention more often
- **Allergies or asthma** in the household make dust and dander control more urgent
- **Condo living** in Toronto often means less square footage but more concentrated dust from HVAC vents and shared building systems
- **High-traffic entryways**, especially in homes with a driveway exposed to winter salt and grime, need more frequent floor and mat cleaning through the colder months
